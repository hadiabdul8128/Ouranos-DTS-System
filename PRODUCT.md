# Ouranos

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Service members completing administrative travel and veterans deciding what to do after military service. Transition's first audience is recently separated veterans seeking work or training.

## Product Purpose

Make a complicated process understandable and give people an obvious next action. Travel prepares vouchers; Transition helps a veteran compare employment, training and education, then act on a chosen path.

## Positioning

Turn information and paperwork into a short, actionable workflow backed by evidence.

## Brand Personality

Simple, clear and trustworthy. Reuse the current quiet workspace, familiar controls and concise language.

## Anti-references

Traditional government websites with dense forms and unnecessary review steps. Complicated AI interfaces and unexplained recommendations.

## Design Principles

- Show the next useful action.
- Ask only what changes the plan.
- Explain uncertainty and preserve the person's choices.
- Use verified resources; do not invent opportunities or eligibility.
- Keep teammates' features modular and shared changes small.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support mobile and keyboard use, readable labels and reduced motion. Offer direct housing support without requiring completion of a career questionnaire.
